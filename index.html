Load signed bills from a list

Upload or paste four columns: Company, Signed Bill No, Sales Person, Amount Outstanding. Each bill is added as Signed, with the outstanding amount as its bill amount. The sales person is kept as history only: nobody sees the bill in their login until you pick a name in the Assigned To column below. Retailer, Date and Area / Beat are left empty; fill them in later in the table. If a bill number is already in the ledger you get an alert and that row is skipped unless you tick it.
